\begin{abstract}
In dialogue exploring possible covariant extensions of the Gravitational Entropy Escrow framework (Whitmer 2026), several large-language-model systems independently proposed an equation of the form $\nabla_\mu J_S^\mu = (2\pi k_B / \hbar)\,\epsilon_{\rm bind}$, designated C8, in which a local entropy 4-current is sourced by the local Tolman--Komar binding-energy density. We document the resolution of this proposal across multiple rounds of analysis. With dimensions accounted for correctly, with the standard energy density of the cosmological constant $\epsilon_\Lambda = \Lambda c^4 / (8\pi G)$, and with the integration time chosen as the light-crossing time of the relevant horizon, C8 reproduces both the Bekenstein--Hawking entropy of a Schwarzschild black hole and the Gibbons--Hawking entropy of the de Sitter cosmological horizon \emph{exactly}. However, this recovery is a consistency check rather than a derivation: C8 with $t = R/c$ is algebraically identical to the saturated Bekenstein bound (Bekenstein 1981), and any horizon that saturates the Bekenstein bound by construction will be reproduced by C8 trivially. The choice of integration time $t = R/c$ is post-hoc; other natural time scales associated with the horizon (Hawking evaporation, inverse surface gravity, free-fall) give different answers ranging across many orders of magnitude. C8 therefore reduces to a rate-equation reformulation of an established 1981 result rather than producing genuinely new physics. We document this resolution --- and the chain of dimensional and convention errors that complicated reaching it --- to spare future researchers extending the escrow framework from the same dead end. The published paper's interpretive content is unaffected; its central open problem (the order-unity coefficient $\alpha \approx 1.34$ between the bare $\Lambda$-form $a_0$ and the empirical Milgrom value) is not addressed by C8 in either direction.
\end{abstract}
